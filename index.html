My Calendar

My Calendar is a personal desktop calendar app for macOS, built and used by its developer only. It is not distributed through any store.

It shows the developer's own Google Calendar events and Google Tasks in a month view with keyboard shortcuts, and lets the developer create, edit, and delete events in Google Calendar. Google remains the only place the data is stored.
